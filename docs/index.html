What you can do

A full voice-typing workflow that stays on your Mac.

⌘Global shortcut dictation

Press ⌘⇧Space anywhere, speak, press again. The transcription pastes into your focused app, with clipboard and history fallback.

📋Clipboard context

Copy text, links, files, or images while recording and they join the composed result — text and links inline, files as local paths.

🔒Encrypted local history

Sessions, audio, and context are stored locally with SQLCipher and per-account keys protected by the macOS Keychain.

📖Personal dictionary

Teach SeaSnail your names and jargon. It can optionally learn from your corrections after pasting — every learned word is undoable.

✨Optional AI cleanup

Off by default. If enabled, only the transcript and dictionary hints go to your own provider — clipboard content is replaced with placeholders first.

🔌OpenAPI & scoped tokens

A local OpenAPI interface with scoped integration tokens and encrypted data export, so your workflow can extend beyond the app.

Privacy is the architecture, not a setting

✓Speech recognition runs on-device with a bundled Sherpa ONNX SenseVoice int8 runtime — your audio never leaves the Mac.
✓Accounts are local. Your password is the key to your data; no online registration, no telemetry.
✓Everything at rest is encrypted: SQLCipher database, AEAD-encrypted files, keys in the macOS Keychain.
✓AI cleanup is opt-in, uses providers you configure, and masks clipboard context before any request.

Early stage, built in the open

SeaSnail is under active development. Current builds are unsigned development builds for macOS 13+ on Apple Silicon — or build from source to see exactly what runs on your machine.
